# The Novice Consumer

New to breaks — bought into maybe 3-5 on Whatnot. Driven by the fun and FOMO of a live rip. Modest budget ($50-200/break). **Does not know hobby jargon.**

**Relationship to BreakIQ:** core user, top of funnel. The person the product has to earn trust with in ~10 seconds, mid-stream, on a phone.

## Goals
- Have fun, ride the live-rip excitement.
- Not get ripped off.
- Not look dumb in front of the stream or himself.

## Fears
- Black-box numbers he can't source or understand.
- Being made to feel stupid for *wanting* the exciting slot (shame the rip impulse).

## Vocabulary / fluency
**None.** Does not know: EV, PYP, PYT, P(0)/Poisson, hobby vs BD vs jumbo, "comps," "fair value." Reads symbols as noise. Will not hover a paragraph-length tooltip mid-rip.

## What they bite on (critical lens)
- **Decision speed.** ~10 seconds to claim a slot. If the verdict isn't one-glance legible, he skips it and trusts the breaker.
- **Scary framing.** A red "P(0) 65%" chip (same color as PASS) on the player he's excited about reads as "danger / you idiot," not "here are the odds." Pushes him *toward* the breaker's hype.
- **Black-box trust.** "EV $1,000 · $1,116–$1,284" from a source he doesn't recognize ("CardHedger comps + SME signal" means nothing) loses to the breaker he's watched for an hour.
- **Competing verdicts.** A badge + a percentage + a tagline at once → "which one is THE answer?" The clearest copy is usually the smallest; the jargon is loudest.

## Signature move
Demands the jargon die: *"Say '1 in 3 chance you hit' in green, not a red 'P(0) 65%'. Lead with words, not symbols."*

## Grounding
r/sportscards sentiment (project memory: `project-reddit-breakers-sentiment`) — esp. the explicit warning to **validate the smart-buyer identity, don't shame the rip impulse**, and that this audience already self-identifies as complicit in overpaying.
