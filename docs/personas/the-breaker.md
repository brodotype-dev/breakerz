# The Breaker

Runs live sports-card breaks (Whatnot / Fanatics Live), buys sealed cases, sells team/player slots to viewers in real time, and makes a living on the margin between case cost and total slots sold.

**Relationship to BreakIQ:** the "antagonist" in the original positioning ("stop overpaying breakers") — but the single most promising distribution channel if we recruit rather than attack.

## Goals
- Maximize revenue per case.
- Sell through *every* slot — especially the weak teams — so the case clears and the break is profitable.
- Keep the stream fun and frictionless; momentum sells slots.

## Fears
- A buyer-side tool that flashes "you're overpaying" mid-stream → arguments in chat, friction on every sale, viewers second-guessing.
- Being branded a fleecer when he's running an honest business with real costs.

## Vocabulary / fluency
Fully fluent. Thinks in case cost, sell-through, "star tax," PYT vs PYP vs random, platform cut, the hybrid-break pattern (carve stars/rookies into their own spots).

## What they bite on (critical lens)
- **EV ≠ auction price.** He doesn't price on expected value — he prices on real-time demand. A Wemby slot goes for 4× "fair" because 200 people want one slot. A model that calls that fleecing doesn't understand live auctions.
- **Cross-subsidy.** Cheap weak-team slots only sell *because* the star slots overcharge to cover the case. Pricing each slot in isolation breaks the economics that let the break exist at all.
- **His real cost stack the model ignores:** platform cut (~8% on Whatnot), shipping every single card, sales tax, his hours streaming, the risk of unsold slots. The model's "Fair" margin is computed on pull-EV, not his breakeven.
- **Variance chips kill impulse buys.** The lottery dopamine is the product; a prominent "no-hit %" cools the exact heat he sells.

## Signature move
Offers the unlock: *"Let me claim the Fair badge — I'd put it in my stream title."* The thing that flips him from enemy to channel.

## Grounding
r/sportscards sentiment (project memory: `project-reddit-breakers-sentiment`) — esp. "collectors are equally to blame for paying the premium" and the pushback against framing breakers as having "ruined" the hobby.
