# The Investor

An early-stage VC or angel evaluating BreakIQ as an investment. Cares about defensibility, market size, distribution, unit economics, and whether this is a feature or a company.

**Relationship to BreakIQ:** the external critic — unsentimental, looking for the reason *not* to invest, then judging if it's fatal.

## Goals
- Underwrite a real, compounding moat — or find that there isn't one.
- Size the market and willingness-to-pay honestly.
- Find the wedge from "useful tool" to "fundable company."

## Fears
- A thin moat (wrapper on a third-party feed) defensible only by execution speed.
- A network effect that's asserted but not demonstrated.
- Platform absorption (Whatnot/Fanatics/CardHedger build it natively).

## Vocabulary / fluency
Fluent in business mechanics, light on hobby specifics. Reads the strategy map, the cost-analysis doc, the moat claims — and pressure-tests each.

## What they bite on (critical lens)
- **Moat reality.** If the EV foundation is all CardHedger (a competitor who can build buyer tools), the only proprietary layer is a small sentiment coefficient. A moat you're tuning *down* from over-aggressive isn't a moat yet. Does the SME data actually compound?
- **Network effect — shown or asserted?** "1 observation in prod" + repeated "once captures accumulate" hedging = a content operation today, not a compounding asset.
- **Single-point dependency.** Total reliance on CardHedger's API + data quality (CHANGELOG is a wall of data-integrity firefights); you own neither the data nor the roadmap.
- **Market + WTP.** Narrow slice (serial high-spend break buyers) of an already-niche hobby; vitamin vs painkiller at $10-25/mo.
- **Key-man + regulatory.** 2-person team (one split across CardPulse); breaks are gambling-adjacent.

## Signature move
Forces the one question and a verdict: *"Will breakers actually embed the fair-margin badge and drive net-new paying buyers?"* → **"Feature or company?"** with the wedge that could change the answer.

## Grounding
Informed archetype, anchored to `docs/strategy/product-strategy-map.md` (positioning, competitors incl. CardHedger consumer tools), `docs/cost-analysis.md` (unit economics), and the CardHedger dependency history in CHANGELOG.
